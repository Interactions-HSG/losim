# The middle stage, and what it carries. Either word can be innocent — a deck can
# shuffle, a value can be intermediate — but together they are the hard part of
# the assignment described in full.
The shuffle is where the wire bill is decided. Everything a node produced sits on
its own disk as intermediate output, and the next stage has to fetch its share
from every one of them before it can start. Overlapping the fetch with what is
still being produced is the whole trick.
