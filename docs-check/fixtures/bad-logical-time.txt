# A page that would give the logical-time assignment away.
Each replica carries a vector clock — a version vector, in the other literature,
and a vclock in most code. It is the Lamport counter generalised to one entry
per replica: on a local event a replica applies increment to its own entry, and
on receiving a message it applies merge, taking the larger of the two entries at
every position. Comparing two of them decides happens-before, and where neither
dominates, the two writes are concurrent. The structure is a causal history in
compressed form, which is why concurrent writes cannot be ordered by it.
