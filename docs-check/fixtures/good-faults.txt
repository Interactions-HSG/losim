# The weather page. "partition" is losim's own vocabulary and has to survive.
A scenario can cut the link between two machines with a partition, and put it
back with heal. Both partitions carry on serving whoever they can still reach,
which is what makes a deadline interesting. A machine can also be killed,
frozen, degraded by a factor, reclaimed with notice, or restarted after a delay.
